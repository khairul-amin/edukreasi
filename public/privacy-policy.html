Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Exam Edu Kreasi Android application and related services operated by EduKreasi collect, process, store, disclose, and protect user information. This page is intended to serve as the public privacy notice for Google Play Store and for users of the app.

Last updated: March 20, 2026

Developer: EduKreasi

Email: examedukreasi@gmail.com

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to the Exam Edu Kreasi Android app, its login and activation flows, QR-based school activation, exam WebView experience, and related web endpoints used to provide exam access, token validation, updates, and operational support.

2. Information We Collect or Process

Depending on how the app is used, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Google account data: email address, display name, and authentication information required for Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.
  • School activation data: QR activation content, school hash, NPSN, spreadsheet or activation URL, local/offline URL, and related configuration values required to connect the app to the correct school environment.
  • Student and exam data: student name, class, email-based matching, exam schedule, exam subject list, status, and links provided by the school or institution through connected data sources.
  • Technical and local app data: network connection state, battery status shown in the interface, WebView session data, cookies, cache, locally stored activation settings, and temporary app diagnostics needed to run the app.
  • Support and operational data: update checks, error states, and basic request metadata needed to keep the service available and secure.

3. Permissions Used by the App

The app may request or rely on the following Android permissions and system capabilities:

  • Camera: used to scan QR codes for school activation and local/offline exam setup.
  • Internet and network access: used for login, activation, token validation, update checks, and loading remote exam resources.
  • Notification permission on supported Android versions: may be requested for operational app notices when such features are enabled.

The app does not request access to contacts, precise location, microphone, call logs, SMS, or media libraries as part of its normal exam flow.

4. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

  • authenticating users through Google Sign-In and related identity services;
  • connecting the app to the correct school configuration through QR activation or local/offline mode;
  • matching a signed-in student email to school-managed student records;
  • displaying exam schedules, Google Form links, and exam status in the app;
  • retrieving or validating school exam tokens where applicable;
  • maintaining service reliability, security, compatibility, and update delivery.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only when required to operate the service, including with:

  • Google services for sign-in, authentication, and school-managed resources such as Google Sheets or Google Forms;
  • Firebase Authentication for account authentication flows used by the app;
  • Supabase endpoints for QR activation and token-related server functions;
  • the school or educational institution that manages the spreadsheet, exam content, and student records;
  • service providers or authorities where disclosure is necessary for legal compliance, security, or service continuity.

6. Local Storage and Retention

The app may store certain data locally on the device, including activation settings, school URL references, NPSN, cached exam data, cookies, and WebView session data.

Locally stored information may remain until the user resets activation, signs out, clears app data, or uninstalls the app. School-managed academic records and cloud-side data may be retained by the school or connected service provider according to their own policies and operational needs.

7. Offline or Local Network Mode

In some cases, the app may be configured to use a school-provided local or offline exam URL after QR activation. In this mode, certain configuration values are stored locally on the device so the app can connect to the approved local exam environment.

8. Security Measures

We use reasonable safeguards to help protect information processed by the app. These measures may include HTTPS-based network access for supported endpoints, authenticated sign-in flows, local cleanup on logout or reset actions, and application hardening techniques appropriate for the platform.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to reduce unnecessary exposure and access to personal information.

9. Children's and Student Privacy

Exam Edu Kreasi is designed for educational use. If the app is used by students or users below the age of majority, that use is expected to occur under the supervision of a school, institution, parent, guardian, or another authorized party, subject to applicable law and school policy.

10. Your Choices and Data Requests

Users may be able to sign out of Google authentication, reset activation data in the app, or clear local device data through app or system settings.

Requests relating to school-managed student data, exam records, or institution-owned content should generally be directed to the relevant school or educational institution. Requests relating to app-level privacy concerns may be sent to the developer contact listed below.

11. No Sale of Personal Data and No Advertising Claim

We do not sell personal data and this app is not designed to use personal data for third-party advertising.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page together with the most recent update date.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or need to submit a privacy-related request, please contact EduKreasi at examedukreasi@gmail.com.
